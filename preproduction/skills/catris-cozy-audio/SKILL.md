# Catris cozy audio

Honor [preparation mode](../../../CATRIS_MASTER_PROMPT.md). Briefs and manifests are allowed now; game audio integration or paid content generation require the production task's scope.

Read [presentation design](../../features/presentation/design.md), [map moods](../../MAPS.md) and [asset briefs](../../ASSET_BRIEFS.md). The reference video has an audio track, but this preparation session did not verify its sound by listening. Do not claim exact soundtrack reproduction.

## Source and mix

Record author/source/license, original master and editing history for every shipped file. Use isolated music, ambience, foley, cat and UI buses with saved settings. Masters should retain headroom; bus gain proposals are not measured loudness targets.

Use natural variations, cooldowns and a small voice limit. Preview grid updates are not audio events. Pickup/snap can be tactile without a meow every time. Avoid extremely short loops, harsh high-frequency rain/chimes, dense music and procedural beeps masquerading as finished cat recordings.

For Unity mixer wiring or import/memory choices, read the bundled `route-audio-to-mixer-groups` or `optimize-audio` skill and version-matched documentation. Confirm the required Editor connection before using their live commands.

## Lifecycle and listening

Check loop seams, crossfades, repeated interactions, pause/resume, headphone/Bluetooth changes and interruptions. Resume exactly one ambience/music instance. Test both iPhone speakers and headphones, including low volume. Report what was actually listened to.

Haptic is optional, event-based and independently disabled: one gentle pickup/snap cue and a restrained solve cue. Unsupported hardware does nothing safely. Haptic feedback must not be the only invalid/valid signal.

Deliver an event-to-file/mixer table, provenance, the tuning decisions and device listening evidence. Mark unacquired/unheard material explicitly instead of calling it finished.
